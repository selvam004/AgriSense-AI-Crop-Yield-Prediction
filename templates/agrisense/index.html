About AgriSense

AgriSense integrates Artificial Intelligence, Real-Time Weather Data, and Soil Analysis to help predict crop yield with precision. 🌾

The system leverages regional climate patterns, soil pH balance, organic carbon levels, and irrigation dynamics to compute high-accuracy yield forecasts. Our goal is to empower farmers with sustainable, data-backed decisions that increase crop productivity while conserving water and land resources 🌍.
